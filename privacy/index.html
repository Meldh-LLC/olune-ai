Privacy Policy

Olune is built so that your private thoughts stay private. This policy describes the few times the app or this website touches a network.

Effective July 27, 2026

Who This Policy Covers

This policy covers the Olune app and olune.ai, both provided by Meldh LLC. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to support@meldh.com.

What Olune Collects

Olune has no accounts, analytics, advertising, trackers, or Meldh-operated inference servers. Meldh does not automatically receive your conversations, Journal entries, notes, settings, or model prompts. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

What Stays on Your Device

Everything you write and save, including conversations, Journal passages, notes, and settings, is stored on your device. Saved material is kept in an encrypted database protected by a key held in the device operating system. Inference and storage work offline after setup.

The One-Time Model Download

The language model that powers Olune is a large file downloaded from a third-party host during setup. The host can see that a download happened and ordinary network information such as the requesting IP address. The download request does not include anything you have written or saved in Olune.

Purchases

If you buy an optional upgrade, the purchase is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Meldh does not operate a purchase server and does not receive your payment-card details. The store may provide the app with a purchase entitlement so it can recognize what you own. The store handles its own data under its privacy policy.

Backups You Choose to Make

Olune can export an encrypted backup protected by a password you choose. The file is created on your device and leaves it only when you choose where to save or share it. Meldh does not receive the backup or its password.

Sharing and Reports You Choose

Share hands selected content to your device's share menu. The destination you select then handles that material under its own terms. Report opens an email draft addressed to support@meldh.com and may include excerpts from the question and response. Nothing is sent until you send the message.

If you contact Meldh, we receive the email address and information in your message. We use it to respond, investigate the issue described, protect the app and its users, and meet legal obligations. Meldh does not use support or report content for advertising.

Links You Open

A link opened from Olune is handled by your browser or the destination app. That destination sees the ordinary network information associated with the visit and applies its own privacy policy. Olune adds no conversation or Journal content to the request unless the link itself clearly represents content you chose to share.

Retention and Deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it through Olune or remove the app. Meldh cannot access or remotely delete material it never receives. Support and report emails are kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond, investigate, maintain necessary business records, or comply with law. You may request deletion of information you sent to Meldh by emailing support@meldh.com. Legal obligations may require limited records to be kept.

This Website

Olune.ai uses no Meldh analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, account system, or marketing cookies. The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process ordinary request information, including IP addresses and browser details, to deliver and secure the site under GitHub's privacy statement. Email links open your email service, whose terms then apply.

Children

Olune is not directed to children under 13 and should not be used by them. Meldh does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent information to Meldh, contact us so it can be addressed.

Security

Olune uses encryption for its local database and password-protected backups. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device, operating system, backup password, and email account protected.

Changes and Contact

Material changes will appear on this page with a revised effective date. For privacy questions or requests, email support@meldh.com.
