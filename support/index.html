Olune Support

Questions, response reports, privacy requests, and purchase issues are welcome at support@meldh.com.

No account or sign-in required

Email support@meldh.com. Include the platform you use, your Olune version, and a short description of the issue. Do not include private conversation text unless it is necessary and you intend to share it.

Report a Response

Use Report beneath the response in Olune. The app prepares an email with the relevant excerpts, and you decide whether to send it.

Privacy Request

Olune has no account and Meldh cannot see local app data. Email us to ask about or delete information you previously sent to Meldh.

Delete Local Data

Delete individual conversations or Journal entries inside Olune. Removing the app deletes its remaining local data under your operating system's normal app-removal process.

Purchases and Refunds

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Restore a purchase through Olune; request a refund through the store that processed it.

Before Olune Is Released

Olune is still in development. Store links, supported-device guidance, and release troubleshooting will be added here after they have been verified.

Urgent Help

Support email is not monitored as an emergency service. If you or someone else may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Additional crisis resources are listed in the Disclaimer.
