Support

Email support@meldh.com for app problems, privacy requests, or purchase issues.

Include your platform, Olune version, and a short description. Share private conversation text only when it is needed to explain the issue.

Report a Response

Tap Report below an Olune response. Review and edit the response, choose a reason, and add an optional note. Send Report submits only those reviewed fields and limited app/model information to reports@meldh.com. It does not attach your question, history, Journal, email address, or device identity.

Privacy and Local Data

Meldh cannot see local app data. Delete conversations and Journal entries in Olune, or remove the app to delete its remaining local data. Email us about information you previously sent to Meldh.

App Store Purchases

Apple or Google handles billing, receipts, and refund requests under its store policy and applicable law. Use the purchase history or support options in the store that processed the transaction.

Before Olune Is Released

Olune is in development. Store links, supported-device guidance, and release troubleshooting will be added here after they have been verified.

Urgent Help

Support email is not an emergency service. If someone may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Additional crisis resources are in the Disclaimer.
