Olune Is a Reflection Tool

Olune is a companion for thinking. It is not a doctor, therapist, counselor, lawyer, financial adviser, or licensed professional, and nothing it says is medical, psychological, legal, or financial advice.

It Is Not for Emergencies

Olune is not built for a crisis and cannot help in one. If you are in danger, thinking of harming yourself or someone else, or facing any emergency, stop using Olune and contact a real person or appropriate emergency service now.

If You Need Someone Now
  • United States: call or text 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
  • Eating-disorder support: contact the National Alliance for Eating Disorders.
  • Outside the United States: find a local service through Find A Helpline.

Use Your Own Judgment

Olune can be wrong, incomplete, or ill-suited to your situation. It draws on traditions of thought to support reflection, but responsibility for your choices remains with you. For decisions involving health, safety, law, money, or another person's welfare, speak with a qualified professional who knows the circumstances.
