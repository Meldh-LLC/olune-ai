Disclaimer

Olune can help you reflect. It cannot know your full circumstances or take the place of a qualified professional.

Effective September 8, 2026

Olune Is a Reflection Tool

Olune generates responses with artificial intelligence. You are not communicating with a human. Olune supports philosophical reflection and does not provide medical, psychological, legal, or financial advice.

It Is Not for Emergencies

Olune cannot provide emergency or crisis care. If you or someone else may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. If you are thinking about suicide or self-harm, contact an appropriate crisis service or qualified professional. Olune may show resource links when it recognizes certain crisis language, but that safeguard can miss indirect or unfamiliar wording.

If You Need Someone Now
  • United States: call or text 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
  • United States: text HOME to 741741 through Crisis Text Line.
  • Eating-disorder support: contact the National Alliance for Eating Disorders.
  • Outside the United States: find a local service through Find A Helpline.

For Adults

Olune is for adults 18 and older. People under 18 may not use it. Olune is not designed or appropriate for minors. Companion chatbots may not be suitable for some minors.

Use Your Own Judgment

Olune can be wrong, incomplete, or ill-suited to your situation. It draws on traditions of thought to support reflection, but responsibility for your choices remains with you. For decisions involving health, safety, law, money, or another person's welfare, speak with a qualified professional who knows the circumstances.
