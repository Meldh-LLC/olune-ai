The experience

Philosophy for the life in front of you.

Olune is a thinking partner for questions without easy answers. Its responses draw from philosophical and psychological traditions while staying with the particulars of your situation.

01

Choose the shape of the conversation

Ask for a natural exchange, several named perspectives, Socratic questions, a direct position, or a slower reflective response. Tone can remain measured, turn warm, or become more challenging.

02

Invite honest resistance

Challenge Me asks one direct question about something you may be taking for granted. Another Perspective returns to the same concern through a different tradition.

03

Keep what matters

Save a passage to your private Journal, add your own note, organize it with tags and collections, or begin a new conversation from it later.

04

Find traditions worth returning to

Explore concise introductions to different schools of thought and use a guided quiz to find the traditions that speak most clearly to your current questions.

Private by construction

Counsel without an audience.

The model, conversation history, and Journal live on your device. Olune has no account system, analytics SDK, advertising, or telemetry.

01

Local inference

After the one-time model download, your conversations are generated on the device in your hands. Prompts are not sent to a cloud model.

02

Encrypted storage

Saved conversations, notes, and settings are kept in an encrypted local database protected by a key held in the device operating system.

03

Deliberate sharing

Nothing reaches Meldh automatically. Content leaves only when you choose an action such as sharing, reporting, or exporting an encrypted backup.

The model download host and any link or sharing destination you choose can see the ordinary network information involved in that action. Olune never includes your conversation or Journal in the model download. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

The Olune Charter

A product that leaves room for a life.

Olune is built for considered use. It has no streaks, attention loops, or pressure to return. The core app remains useful without an account, subscription, or continuing content promise.

01 The Standard model, every response type and tone, conversation, and Journal stay free.
02 Optional paid features are bought once and owned. There are no subscriptions.

04 What ships is complete. A purchase does not depend on promised future work.
05 Progress only counts upward. Olune does not punish a quiet week.

Questions

What to know before Olune arrives.

Is Olune therapy?

No. Olune is a companion for reflection and is not a doctor, therapist, counselor, lawyer, or financial adviser. It is not built for emergencies and cannot replace a qualified professional who knows your circumstances.

Does Olune need an internet connection?

Core conversations and storage work offline after a one-time model download. Opening links, using an app-store purchase, sending a report, or sharing content through another service requires the connection used by that action.

Why is the model download large?

Olune runs a full language model locally so your prompts do not need to travel to a cloud inference service. Exact download sizes and supported device guidance will be published before release.

Can I move my Journal to another device?

Olune can create a password-protected encrypted backup that you choose where to store. Meldh never receives the file or the password.

When will Olune be available?

Olune is in active development for Android and iOS. Release information and verified device requirements will appear here when the app is ready.
