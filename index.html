How Olune works

01

Write in your Journal

Create entries in a private writing space, organize them, and return to your own words without loading the model.

02

Open a reflection

Start an optional AI dialogue from a question or passage. Choose a response style, challenge an assumption, or examine another perspective.

03

Keep and revisit

Save useful passages separately from your own entries, add notes, and begin a new reflection from writing you select.

Privacy

The model, conversations, and Journal live on your device.

01

Local inference

After the one-time model download, conversations are generated on your device. Prompts are not sent to a cloud model.

02

Encrypted storage

Conversations, Journal entries, notes, and letters are kept in an encrypted local database protected by the device operating system.

03

Deliberate sharing

Content leaves only when you choose to share, report, or export an encrypted backup.

Network services used for downloads, links, or sharing can see the ordinary connection information involved in that action. Olune does not include your conversations or Journal in a model download. Read the Privacy Policy.

Product model

Olune is built for considered use. It has no streaks, advertisements, analytics, or pressure to return.

01 The on-device model, every response style and tone, dialogue, and Journal stay free.
02 Optional paid features are one-time purchases.
03 No account is required.
