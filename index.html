How Olune works

01

Choose a response style

Ask for a conversation, several perspectives, Socratic questions, a direct position, or a slower reflection. You can also set the tone.

02

Challenge the frame

Challenge Me questions an assumption. Another Perspective approaches the same concern through a different tradition.

03

Keep what matters

Save a passage to your private Journal, add a note, organize it, or return to it in a later conversation.

Privacy

The model, conversations, and Journal live on your device.

01

Local inference

After the one-time model download, conversations are generated on your device. Prompts are not sent to a cloud model.

02

Encrypted storage

Conversations, notes, and settings are kept in an encrypted local database protected by the device operating system.

03

Deliberate sharing

Content leaves only when you choose to share, report, or export an encrypted backup.

Network services used for downloads, links, or sharing can see the ordinary connection information involved in that action. Olune does not include your conversations or Journal in a model download. Read the Privacy Policy.

Product model

Olune is built for considered use. It has no streaks, advertisements, analytics, or pressure to return.
